# Our First Anniversary 💗

A single self-contained website — your photo and shayari are already embedded inside it,
so there's nothing else to install or link up.

## How to open it

1. Find **index.html** in this folder.
2. Double-click it (or right-click → Open With → your browser: Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
3. That's it — it opens straight in the browser, no internet connection needed.

To send it to her, just share the **index.html** file itself (e.g. AirDrop, WhatsApp as a document,
email attachment, or a USB/drive link). She only needs to double-click it on her own phone or
computer to open the experience.

## What's inside the experience

1. A soft opening question and a glowing button that opens a pair of doors.
2. The anniversary reveal, with a gentle "yes/no" moment.
3. A little private quiz about your Delhi memory (case-insensitive, unlimited tries).
4. A luxury-style anniversary card that opens with a 3D animation.
5. The final page: your photo on one side, your shayari on the other, exactly as you wrote it.

There's also a small ♪ button in the top-right corner for optional background music, plus a
few chime sounds when doors and the card open. All sound is off unless she taps the button.

### Adding "Can't Help Falling in Love" (or any song)

I can't embed that recording directly — it's still under copyright, so I'm not able to
reproduce it. But the site is already wired to use it if you supply your own copy:

1. Get a legally-owned copy of the song as an **.mp3** file (from a purchase you already made —
   iTunes/Apple Music download, a CD you own and ripped, etc.).
2. Rename that file to exactly **song.mp3**.
3. Put it in the **same folder** as `index.html` (right next to it).
4. Open (or re-open) `index.html` and tap ♪ — it will now play your song on loop.

If `song.mp3` isn't there, the button quietly falls back to a soft original ambient hum instead,
so nothing breaks either way.

## Notes

- Everything (photo + styling + code) lives inside **index.html** — one file, easy to share.
- It's fully responsive, so it looks good on her phone as well as on a laptop.
- If you ever want to swap the photo or edit the wording, open index.html in any text editor —
  search for `letter-text-side` to find the shayari text, or `data:image/jpeg;base64,` to find
  where the photo is embedded.
